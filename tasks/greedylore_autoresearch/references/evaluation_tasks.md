# Evaluation tasks and compression policy
The recorded evaluation contract is authoritative. Contract v2 separates **Development experiments** from **Final evaluation**; saved v1 runs retain their original behavior and references. Never relabel historical results or invent reference hashes. New-contract scoring requires newly established matching dense/Root evidence; historical seed results are not authoritative references.

Establish matching dense references and reevaluate Root after a budget/contract change; do not reuse old scores by replacing their hash. Saved runs retain their recorded contract and budgets.
## Candidate-owned method adaptation
- Commit `method_policy.py` with `configure_compression(args, workload)` and implement the method through the shared communication hook. Define the per-workload compression settings/mapping, including any state-dependent policy, before Final evaluation; verify support for matrix and convolution gradients.
- Stock GreedyLoRE vision rank 64, dense prefix 4000 updates and refresh gap 1200 are method defaults only. They are not fixed constraints on candidate rank, compression start, refresh, communication or adaptive policy. Declare candidate choices and effective timing cycles/events consistently.
- The policy receives compression parameters only; returning training fields is rejected. Dense references keep compression disabled. Candidate policy must not change fixed data, training budget, optimizer, seed, precision or evaluation settings.
## Final evaluation and recovery
- `research finish` requires completed passing development validity/quality/resource evidence only. Development timing remains optional. Freeze the selected SHA/design/policy/workload mapping first; run fresh mandatory final timing and both final quality tasks afterward, without reopening development or tuning from feedback.
- Fixed timing uses Llama130M/AdamW, BF16 and FP32, four GPUs, batch 1 per GPU, the contract’s repetitions and complete-cycle policy. Preserve timing validity, event and hardware-comparability checks. Timing magnitude introduces no new promotion gate.
- Optional development timing has cumulative limits of 5 completed / 15 total executions. Mandatory final timing is separate and cannot reuse development measurements. Completed final-component results may be reused only to recover that same frozen evaluation.
- Default infrastructure retry limit is 2 per final component; retain other completed components. Vision retries start from scratch, with no exact checkpoint-resume claim. Retain failures and all measured results. All final components must be terminal or explicitly skipped before independent Analysis and quality-gated Engineering start.
- Final results describe transfer/robustness of a development-selected method. Their reuse by later Nodes makes them available research evidence rather than independent campaign-level held-out evidence.
