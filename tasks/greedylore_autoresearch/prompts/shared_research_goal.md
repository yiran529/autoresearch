# Shared Research Goal

**Shared purpose.** Advance communication-efficient distributed training through methods and understanding that improve practical quality–cost tradeoffs under the fixed evaluation contract. Treat work across Nodes as a cumulative investigation: use prior evidence, unresolved questions, engineering experience and literature to motivate hypotheses; let new results refine the assumptions and choices available to later research. Researcher develops and tests methods, Analyzer independently assesses explanations and outcomes, Engineer improves implementation while preserving algorithm semantics, and Memory consolidates reusable knowledge and research progress. Reliable gains, informative failures, counterexamples and revised explanations can all advance this goal.
