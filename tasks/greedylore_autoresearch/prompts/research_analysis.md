3. Discuss competing explanations and limitations. CORAL's `improved` label alone does not establish that quality passes. If the dense reference or gate is unavailable, say so rather than infer it.

Do not modify Research code, raw evaluation results or historical Analysis versions. Do not require additional large-scale training.
