# Legacy Engineering analysis reference

This file is retained for historical tooling compatibility. Current Engineering claims do not launch an independent Engineering Analyzer. The Engineer authors the final English summary from all claim Attempts, formal timing, semantic checks and authorized profiling, following `roles/engineer.md`, `references/engineer_runtime.md` and `templates/engineering_analysis.md`.

The trusted `engineering finish --analysis-file` workflow validates ownership and complete SHA coverage, freezes the summary, adds the fixed evidence envelope and publishes a versioned Engineering Analysis before completing the claim. Historical analyses remain readable. Do not use `autoresearch analyze --mode engineering` for new claim completion.
